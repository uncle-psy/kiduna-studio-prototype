## Complete narrative

Diversify invites you to widen your interests and listen beyond a single point of view. Restricting yourself too narrowly can close off opportunities; curiosity, adaptability, and openness allow the world's varied strength to move through you. The work is not to scatter your energy, but to marshal it and direct it with purpose.

Rebis—the double thing—embodies Two in One: opposites joined without erasing their difference. The square and compass bring discipline, measure, and useful limits. The seven planets surround and connect to the figure, representing the variety of influences that continually shape a life and the originality that can arise when those forces are consciously integrated.

The dragon is imperfect matter and immense potential: powerful, responsive, and dangerous when handled carelessly. Beneath it, the winged globe suggests completion already present in possibility. The square of Four and triangle of Three call for method, pacing, humility, and unity; the egg protects the work from uncontrolled positive and negative fluctuations so transformation can proceed in its own time.

Mars–Neptune supplies enthusiasm, imagination, and strong feeling, but it requires selection and direction. Avoid vanity, disappointment, chemical escape, and anxiety about what cannot be controlled. Jasper grounds the card's openness and change, supporting courage, steadiness, trust, and a sustainable pace. In the Puffer grade, experimentation is welcome—provided each experiment teaches you how to focus your growing power.

## Card description

A hermaphroditic figure marked Rebis stands upon a fire-breathing dragon. A serpent crouches on a winged globe below. The figure holds a square and compass while the seven planets extend around and connect to the body. Everything is enclosed in the alchemical egg, beyond which positive and negative forces pulse.
