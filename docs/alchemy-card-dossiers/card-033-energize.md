## Complete narrative

Grade: initiate, keyword energize.

The picture shows the mercurial spirit of the pri-primer material. In the shape of a salamander frolicking in the fire. The salamander is deep purple, with green feet, and with a broad green stripe running down its back, in which there are in which there are spaced small golden stars. According to Paracelsus, the salamander lives in the essential spirit fire of nature. Yakob Boehme called the fire the fire crack, the lighting that originates in the hea...saliter, which is the seed of the entire Godhead. The picture has the element of one. You can be a ball of energy, when you wish to be.

You energize others just by watching you, just by them watching you. You can be hyperactive to the point where you exhaust those who try to keep up with you. Ideas flow into your head so that you want to do everything at once. You feel there is so much to do and so little in so little time in which to do it. Try to energize others with your ideas rather than by your actions. You're a nucleus, the central point... Part about which all else revolves. You have an ability to direct and to delegate. Develop that ability. Your place is in management.

The color of the suit is purple, the color associated with power intention, also with ambition. You have the ability to take the heat. Criticism may hurt you, as it does most people, but you are able to hide that hurt. To others it appears as though you have a hard skin. Keep up that appearance, don't let it be known if you are sensitive. Keep a strong outward appearance. In this way, you will be able to inspire and energize others. Take the time to judge the character of others and don't jump to hasty conclusions. The element of one is here all powerful. It is the sun and its fiery energy.

It is creative thought, leadership, and self-determination. Egotism and cynicism must be guarded against, but one must be acknowledged as a number of actions, courage, and daring.

Planetary conjunction: moon, Mars. This is a powerful combination, giving authority without causing resentment. At this time, you are well able to deal with both individuals and with very large groups. But beware developing a short temper. Much like the moon and the sun working together, the moon and Mars give a nice balance of male and female energies. Try to avoid recklessness and ignoring... Future results as present actions, look ahead and try to judge where your actions will lead.

Stone: ruby. The ruby is so named because of its color, from the Latin, rubeus. It is a stone of integrity. It is also the stone that was thought to reflect the spark of life. It is the stone of regeneration, of protection, and it promotes good health. It was once believed that to lay a ruby on a wound would stop the bleeding.

Grade: This card is the Initiate grade
