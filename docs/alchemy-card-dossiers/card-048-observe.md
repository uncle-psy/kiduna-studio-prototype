## Complete narrative

Number three, observe. Element air, ruled by Mercury. The main illustration on this wild card is of Johann Conrad Barkhausen's Elementa Chemies. The secondary illustration is based on the Druid animal oracle card raven by Bill Worthington, though here redrawn as a crow. The key illustration shows sulfur as the sun and Mercurius is the moon, here male and female. Together they bridge the eternal river. Through this contact, Mercury attains the power of fertilizing earth. The secondary illustration is of the crow, observer all that goes on in the world.

Crows and other Corvidae are one of the most intelligent of all animals. They observe and remember. Observe is the key wu-word. This card suggests that you are frequently in a position where you can observe the development of actions by a variety of people. Observation is knowledge. Rather than acting hastily in immediate response to events, it would be hew view to take some time to observe all that is involved. You will then be in a much better position to decide on the best course of action.

Take your time, let both the masculine and the feminine sides of you come into play so that you are totally unbiased and similarly totally sympathetic to the actions of all. The crow, sacred bird of Phoebus Apollo, is a gatherer of information. Flying around the world to see and hear. Variously described as crows or ravens, the two companions of Odin were Thugan, thought, and Munin, memory, who every morning flew over Midgard and reported back what they observed. Air element. This is the power of movement. It naturally ties in with flight, be it of the mind, the body of birds, or the astral essence.

It is the energy and drive of the mind. Mercury correspondence:.
