## Complete narrative

The Cards shelter bloodstone Dh 4: SHELTER

Keyword: Shelter. Gifts: Nurturing. Loving. Accepting. Challenges: Gullibility. Too trusting. Recklessness. (Frankfort, 1622). The illustration is from Johann Daniel Mylius's Philosophia reformata

The picture shows a Sun-headed Earth, as prima materia, suckling a sun child. According to Paracelsus, "The mother of the elements and of all created things... Nurse is the earth." The figure stands in a mountain stream. On the top of a cliff to the left of the picture is a salamander. On a cliff to the right of the picture are two eagles in a nest. The elements of One and Two are present. To offer SHELTER to those in need is a commendable act. Even when you have little yourself you tend to offer what you have, to help those even 1I8 Egg Suit less fortunate.

This is a wonderful trait but you need to be slightly more circumspect. There are many who would take advantage of your good nature, to your detriment. Even as you 'suckle" one, there are two or more looking on with jealousy, demanding attention. The color of this suit is purple, which is associated with tension, also with power and ambition. The stream in the picture-the running water-symbolizes spirituality. It may well be that you are comforting others with your spiritual beliefs. Certainly you are rooted in your spirituality. It is this that gives you much of your strength.

You are, or should be, very open with no prejudice, no bigotry, no antagonism of any sort toward others. The salamander is the fire from which will come new birth; a new beginning, That fire may be a low heat or of a high intensity. So may run your feelings: soft and low, or fierce and hot. You need to be more aware of your feelings, and your reactions to people and situations. If you have a temper that can become quickly inflamed, learn to assuage it and bring it under control. It can be useful, but only under the right circumstances. The eagles are symbols of knowledge and inspiration.

Many times they signify the ending of the work. Here they rest in a nest, representing the home. Home is shelter. It is security. To wherever you may fly, there is no joy like returning home. It is where you function best.

Planetary conjunction: MOON-SATURN There is a difficulty expressing emotions at this time. There is also a concern over something that was done in the past. Now is the time to let that go; to move on. You have the ability to overcome emotional whims. You can be serious, though sometimes a little too serious. You take very little for granted, examining every iota of a subject. At this time you feel that the world revolves around you. Others do not necessarily share that view. Give a little and step back to see the big picture.

Stone: BLOODSTONE The bloodstone, or heliotrope, is a variety of green jasper. It is called bloodstone because of the distinctive red specks with which it is impregnated. These are actually caused by the iron oxide in the stone. 119 The Cards It reduces both mental and emotional stress. It can also promote the healer within you, by stimulating the kundalini. Bloodstone was worn as an amulet by the Gnostics, in the belief that it would prolong their life. Worn around the neck, it is said to stop nose bleeding, however severe. In your working to help others, wear a bloodstone as an amulet.

Hold it in your hand and concentrate your energies into it for three minutes or more, at least once a day. Also hold it when sending mental images to help others.

Grade: This card is in the Initiate grade... You have traveled a distance along the path and are no longer a beginner. This means you have responsibilities. You already have experience on which you may draw. Yet bear in mind that you do not yet know all that needs to be known. You still have much to learn. Carefully guard those secrets you have uncovered, for they will stand you in good stead in the time to come. The symbol for this grade is the balance. Balance, then, what you know against what you need to know. Acknowledge your ignorance while enjoying your knowledge.
