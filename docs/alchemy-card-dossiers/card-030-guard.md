## Complete narrative

this card indicates that this is a time to guard against others' ignorance by keeping silent. With the present climate, it would also behoove you to guard your possessions. They do not have to be kept under lock and key, but do not carelessly leave items lying around needlessly. There are those who are jealous of what you have, and what you have achieved, and would not hesitate to avail themselves of what you have worked hard to acquire. You hold in your hands those things that are attractive to others; things that others have long desired.

The world may be at your feet, and you may be crowned with all that is good and worthwhile, but now is not the time to let down your guard. One is the number of the Sun. It stands for individuality, leadership, and self-confidence. Acknowledge the desire to give, and to share. Above all, take the time to do things thoroughly and completely. The One is reinforced with the silver goblet (metal of the Moon) containing the fires of your energy. The red eagle of the crown is a symbol of freedom colored by courage and strength.

The eagle exalted, as on the crown, is an alchemical symbol for Mercury in his most sublime state. It is an emblem of knowledge and inspiration. The looseness of the hair is a sign of the freedom that struggles to get out. Two is a true balance of male and female energies; the yin-yang of life. The Sun is assertion and rulership, while the Moon is receptivity and personality. Both are at your feet. The number Two is also reinforced by the mighty wings, which enclose you in their protection.

Planetary conjunction: MOON-NEPTUNE You need to develop your insight into other people; to learn to judge them not just from appearances. You believe strongly in yourself, which is good. But don't get so lost in yourself that you ignore all others. You tend to let your imagination run away with you at times, and you have a I16 Egg Suit very active imagination. You can be highly sensitive but you try not to let it show when you are hurt. You have natural mediumistic tendencies, which should be developed.

Stone: JASPER This stone is one of the gems of Aries and is usually found as dark green, with flecks of red. It was a favorite ornament of the ancient Egyp tians. They always wore the jasper on the thumb, since the thumb is ruled by Mars and Mars is the ruling planet of Aries. The Egyptian pharaoh Nechepsos wore a jasper engraved in the form of a dragon. It is a stone that will aid you in your openness, in your ability to change.

It is not as commonly used for a talisman as are many other stones, but it can be a particularly potent gem and, for you at this time, can help you develop trust and help you pace yourself.

Grade: This card is in the Puffer grade-you are the apprentice, with much to learn. In all that has been said, above, remember that you are at the beginning and have a long road to travel. Pace yourself and, especially, learn from your experiences.

## Card description

A naked young woman stands with arms spread on the Sun and Moon. Long curling hair falls almost to her knees and a pair of large wings frames her. Her left hand holds a combined Sun-Moon emblem; her right holds a silver goblet containing fire. A gold crown topped by a red eagle rests on her head.
