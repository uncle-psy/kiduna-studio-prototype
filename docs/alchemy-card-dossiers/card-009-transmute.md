## Complete narrative

To transmute is to alter in form, appearance, or nature, to transform or convert. This is the perfect time in your life for making a complete change, be it, of lifestyle, occupation, location, religion, marital status, or whatever. Throughout your life, there's a throughout your life, there's seldom the opportunity to completely redirect yourself, but this card indicates that now is the ideal time. Do not hesitate to make a complete change, to take completely different job, to move somewhere you have never been before, to join a group, or take up a hobby that you have never experienced before.

This is the time for the union of opposites, the joining of fire and water produces steam. But steam is a powerful, and useful thing. It can be a most powerful driving force. Beware, however, since steam can also burn. Exercise caution, yet don't let that stop you from moving forward. The elements of one, two, four, and eight, are apparent in this figure, as are the elements of fire and water. The combining of the two great forces. Introduces the one, which is the all-powerful, creative, dominating figure. There is here leadership, pride, aggressiveness, creativity, and initiative.

It is made up of the two, which modifies the dominating figure, by bringing in a female element, of passivity and diplomacy. One is the sun, and two is the moon, the perfect couple. Each of the figures of the picture possesses four hands. Four is tied to routine and discipline. It also indicates ability for detail work, and for anything mechanical. But with two sets of hands, there is the number eight to be considered. Eight is the number of nobility, and piety, it shows a generosity, and helplessness of the individual.

There's also a fondness for money, yet the ability to deal with money, especially in large amounts. The element of fire is a purifier. It can be destructive, but also cleansing and energizing. In addition to being a destroyer, it is also a creator. As fire is a purifier, so water is a cleanser. It is connected with healing, and with spirituality. It is soothing and loving. Both of these elements are vital to our existence. Know them, and respect them.

Planetary conjunction: Venus, Moon. Although this conjunction indicates a lack of the ability to express emotion, there is a real sense of appreciation for beauty. It indicates a calmness of emotions, with no inclination toward hysterics at any time. You should guard against mood swings, and try not to be too changeable. Judgment can be weak at times, and there is a need to be on the watch for gullibility. With these two planets, you are likely to be popular, often leading to over-assertiveness. You have an easy way of winning over people to your way of thinking.

Stone: turquoise, a stone once used in rituals to incite love to incite love and passion. Turquoise is clo-closely linked to Venus. It helps transmit energy from one to another, and is a stone that aids communication. It also brings peace of mind, promotes loyalty, and has a tremendous calming influence
