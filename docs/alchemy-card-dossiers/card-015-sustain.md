## Complete narrative

. Number six, sustain.

The picture shows a pelican with its wings spread on its nest, nourishing its young with its own blood. The nest is comprised of intertwined branches, and lined with soft grasses.

The picture contains the elements of one, and three. Sustain yourself, and perhaps more importantly to you, those who are near and dear to you. There are those who depend upon you, and you feel it is your duty to help sustain them. Some of them may be mature enough that they could, in fact, now look after themselves, but you see yourself, in the position of provider, and find it hard to relinquish that position. What you are doing, and have done, is praiseworthy, but it may be time to step back, and review the situation.

In the long run, it is most important that you sustain yourself, if only so that you will always be there, should others really need you. Is this a home situation, or a job situation? Are those who seem so dependent on you, your family members, or are they co-workers? In either case, perhaps you're actually doing themselves a disservice, by too much caring, and nurturing. The spread wings of the pelican indicate a desire to encompass all, to do even more than you are doing. Is this wise? Is it possible to overextend yourself? Yet you are a very good homemaker, office manager, supervisor, or whatever.

You have built a good and solid foundation, and lined it well. You are truly loved, and appreciated, but beware of those who take advantage of your generosity. Remember, sustain yourself first. The number one, is the center of the universe, of your universe it seems. It is an orb of energy and creation, but there are limits even to number one. Learn, and know those limits. Never be afraid to accept help from others if offered. You do not have to carry all burdens yourself. Number three is an instrument of expression. It is energetic, enth-usiastic, and stimulating.

The people you help, certainly seem to stimulate you, leading to more sharing, and self-sacrifice on your part. Know your limits.

Planetary conjunction: Sun Mars. The Sun and Mars are two powerful planets. Together they give tremendous energy and ambition to you. There is a need for constant release of that energy. There is a feeling of power through action. You have great faith in your abilities, but you must become more aware of your limitations, for you cannot always win. You must learn to exercise self-control.

The yellow variety of this stone was used in the Middle Ages to increase physical desire.
