delegate ruby Ơ 4 5: DELEGATE

Keyword: Delegate. Gifts: Generosity. Desire to share. Delegation of duties. Challenges: Judgment of character. Trust. The illustration is from the eighteenth-century manuscript La Sagesse des anciens.

The picture shows a king—possibly sick—seated on his throne. From his heart, six crowns are extended to his planet children. The king holds, in his hands, the orb and scepter of his office and wears the royal crown on his head. An aura, or Gloria, extends from the area around the king and a cauldron of fire is beneath him. The planet children-three male and three female—reach out to accept the offered crowns. Peder Mänsson said (in the sixteenth century) "There are seven kinds of ore...

But 152 Athanor Suit the alchemists want to prove that there is only one ore, namely gold: for it is pertect and the other six are on the way to pertection, to becoming gold!"The elements of One, Six, and Seven are evident in the picture. DELEGATE whenever possible. You tend to take on too much responsibility and, through doing so, make yourself miserable. You have accomplished much but can accomplish much more by not trying to do it all yourself. There are those close to you who would be willing to take some of your responsibility.

You must learn to judge them; to see who is most capable of meeting your standards. You are honored and respected, but there are some who are jealous of your achievements. At this time you are feeling stressed. Don't allow yourself to be pressured You may have favorites but, for now at least, treat all equally until you see how they handle themselves. Keep the main power in your own hands, but relax the reins enough to give yourself some needed respite. One is the number of the ruler; the leader. It is also the number of action, so you need to act now rather than put off doing anything.

There is courage and determination in the number One. Six lives with the guidance of the heart, and is affected by parental teachings of right and wrong, truth, justice, and even religion. There is service in the number but also obstinacy and tardiness. Seven is a number of rewards, so you can here see benefits —for all—from delegating duties. With the number Seven, life is not always simple. It contains dignity and pride but also suspicion and repression.

Planetary conjunction: MARS-JUPITER You can be open and forthright, with strong willpower and enthusiasm, causing many doors to open to you. However, you need to keep yourself from trusting people too much; from becoming gullible. Try to curb any impulsiveness. If you carefully think things through, assessing the end results, you can make excellent decisions. You are very capable in most fields, with a high energy level. But you can get too enthusiastic and, thereby, too optimistic. You are very creative and you tend to draw others to you. You do well teaching your beliefs to others. 153 The Cards

Stone: RUBY The ruby is so named because of its red color (from the Latin Rubeus). It is a stone of integrity. It is also the stone that was thought to reflect the spark of life. It is a stone of regeneration, of protection, and it promotes good health. It was once believed that to lay a ruby on a wound would stop the bleeding.

Grade: This card is in the Initiate grade... You have traveled a distance along the path and are no longer a beginner. This means you have responsibilities. You already have experience on which you may draw. Yet bear in mind that you do not yet know all that needs to be known. You still have much to.learn. Carefully guard those secrets you have uncovered, for they will stand you in good stead in the time to come. The symbol for this grade is the balance. Balance, then, what you know against what you need to know. Acknowledge your ignorance while enjoying your knowledge.
