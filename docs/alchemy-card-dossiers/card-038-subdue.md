## Complete narrative

The picture shows a wild man riding, taming a unif...corn. The animal rears up from a rock on which it stands. On the ground below is a long-legged bird. The wild man was a common figure in medieval times, particularly in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. Wild men may still be seen as supporters for the armorial bearings of such families as La Rochefoucauld, a sixteenth century German manuscript shows a wild man running with a small tree over a shoulder. Tied to the tree is a young man. Wild hairy men are sculpted into the main portal of the fifteenth century's church of San Gregorio, in Valladalid, Spain.

In many, if not most, of the medieval festivals across Europe, the wild man was a traditional figure. A fourteenth-century ivory jewel casket is carved with a scene of a knight in armor rescuing a maiden from a wild man. While the wild man symbolizes harshness, lust, and lack of control, the unicorn is the opposite, representing purity and innocence. Is the unicorn being subdued by the wild man, or is the wild man being subdued by the unicorn? There's plenty of balance of opposites, a dichotomy of rider and mount. Both may be viewed as wild and unruly, and both may need to be subdued.

The card indicates that there may well be a similar dichotomy within you, a wildness purity, a violence gentleness, which swings first one way and then the other. The combination of opposites is reflected in the bird, which carries the color of fire and water. Obviously, the concept of the union of opposites is important in this card. With natural hostility needing to be overcome and subdued. Then it could be overcome is obvious from the picture, where there is a balance and a mutual respect in evidence. Watch out for strong inner urges that you know to be unhealthy.

Know that you can subdue whatever may present itself. You have the inner strength to do what you know to be best for you.

Planetary conjunction: Mars, Uranus. Although you may tend to appear cold and impersonal to others at times, you have a strong personality and are very capable. At present, you must not let your heart rule your head. You need to be in charge, to examine your needs and desires, and to look forward to the possible consequences of your actions. This is a time of high, intense energy, which must be strict...which must be kept under strict control. You have the ability to make quick decisions, but would be advised to take more time than you think, necessary. You may balk against routine, but some routine is necessary.

Stone: bloodstone. The bloodstone, or heliotrope, is a variety of green jasper. It is called bloodstone because of the distinctive red specks with which it is impregnated. These are actually caused by the iron oxide in the stone. It reduces both mental and emotional stress. It can also promote the healer within you by stimulating the Kundalini. Bloodstone was worn as an amulet by the Gnostics, in the belief that it would prolong their life. Worn around the neck, it is said to stop nosebleeding, however severe. In your working to help others, wear the bloodstone as an amulet.

Hold it in your hand and concentrate your energies into it for three minutes or more, at least once a day. Also hold it when sending mental images to help others.

Grade: the card is in the puffer grade

## Card description

A wild man rides and tames a unicorn. The unicorn rears from a rock on which it stands, while a long-legged bird occupies the ground below. The opposed wild man and unicorn embody harshness and uncontrolled force balanced against purity and innocence; the bird carries the colors of fire and water.
