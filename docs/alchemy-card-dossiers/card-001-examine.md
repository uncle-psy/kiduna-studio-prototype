## Complete narrative

Examine is the keyword, and therefore, this card is an indicator that something in your life, needs to be closely looked at this time. It may be something you have been putting off for a long time, in which case, put it off no more. The time has come. Blue, the color of this suit, is the color of changeability. A dark blue can mean depression and impulsiveness, while a light blue is patience and understanding. There are three numbers of significance apparent in the picture, two, three, and four. Two is the number of balance and union, but two can repel as well as attract.

In this instance, however, the two is composed of male and female, a perfect balance. The four is made up strongly from these two human figures. They are positioned to represent the four elements, represented by the crow, the eagle, the hedgehog, and the swan. In numerology, the number four is a practical number. The number of order and stability. It is the number of individualists, seemingly not greatly concerned with material matters, who are not easily swayed by money. They may, however, be good at handling finances.

It indicates that there can be sudden, unexpected feelings of loneliness and isolation, that in turn, can lead to despondency and melancholy. You may have a hard time making friends at this time, but they are fiercely loyal when you do. Three is a number of foundation. It is the basis of the tripod on which sat the oracle at Delphi. It has for millennia been regarded as a magical number. In numerology, this number indicates a tendency to rise to positions of authority, seemingly with little effort. You could well excel in positions of trust and authority. However, beware of becoming dictatorial.

## Card description

Two figures, one female and one male, balance with animals on their heads and beneath their feet. The woman has a swan on her head and stands on a hedgehog; the man has an eagle on his head and balances a crow on his feet. Both extend their arms for balance. Beneath them is a circle containing three hearts emitting forms that may be serpents or the long necks and beaked heads of swans.
