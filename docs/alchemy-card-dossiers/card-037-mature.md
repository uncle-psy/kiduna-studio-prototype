## Complete narrative

Mature asks power to become seasoned rather than merely forceful. Bravery is joined with the ability to learn and to teach: experience has value when it remains open, can be tested, and can be passed on without hardening into superiority.

The stag and unicorn meet within the forest-body. The stag carries soul and Mercurial intelligence; the unicorn carries spirit and sulphur. One appears in the unicorn's single horn, while Two appears in the stag's antlers and in the pair themselves. Maturity emerges as singular purpose learns to live in relationship.

The card's challenges—excessive trust, egotism, and loneliness—show what happens when power becomes isolated or unexamined. Mars–Pluto intensifies the will to transform; Agate steadies that force through grounded strength and balance. In the Puffer grade, the work is still experimental: learn by doing, and let every lesson make your power more responsible.

## Card description

A stag, symbolizing soul and Mercury, approaches a unicorn, symbolizing spirit and sulphur, in a forest representing the body. The unicorn has a golden horn and golden hooves, and a large pool lies behind the two figures.
