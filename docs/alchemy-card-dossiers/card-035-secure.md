Keyword: Secure. Gifts: Security. Gratitude. Serenity. Inner balance. Freedom from fear. Challenges: Resolving inner conflicts. Overcoming loneliness. Showing appreciation. The illustration is from the Viatorium Spagyricum of Herbrandt Jamsthaler (Frankfort on the Main, 1625).

The picture shows the image of Mercurius senex exhaling the spiritus and anima, or spirit and soul, in the form of angels. Mercurius senex is Saturn, in alchemy representative of "moist" vapors (combining with the "dry" vapors of sulphur, the Greeks and later followers believed these to be the composition of all metals). The raven, symbol of the nigredo, is perched on the old man. Nigredo, or blackness, is the initial state of the substance, in alchemy. The figures are enclosed in the protective egg; the 130 Egg Suit womb or alembic.

Outside the egg are the opposing forces of Sun/Sol (radiating immense heat) and Wind/Aolus (blowing with full force). Above are the Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, and Saturn. It contains the elements of Four, Seven, and Two; the four figures in the sphere surrounded by the seven planets plus the elements of sun and wind SECURE yourself by establishing your boundaries and building the protective egg of spiritual energy all about you. The meaning of this suit is protection. Relax in the knowledge that you are secure.

You are now at a stage where you need have no fear of physical or metaphysical actions against you. You are well protected. Actions may take place nearby but you will remain untouched. The forces of the Sun and the Moon together, with five powerful planets, watch over you. You are now at a beginning stage in the process of passing on the knowledge you have acquired. The time is ripe to teach others; to breathe the spirit of your teachings into those who come into contact with you. The old man is the survivor of many years of conflict. He is now safe and secure; finally able to relax.

The angels that are soul and spirit are watching over him, while the raven of sudden, violent action is at his beck and call, now a servant of the master. The egg of protection surrounds and encompasses the man, keeping at bay all that would harm him. (Remember, the Egg of Protection is the symbol of this suit.) Outside, the elements battle: the Sun blazes forth with intense heat against the gale-force of the winds, directed in freezing intensity. Above, the Sun and Moon, in company with the planets, look on approvingly. Of the planets, Saturn is shown in its negative aspect.

Beware, therefore, of selfishness, meanness; of being narrow-minded and dogmatic. ## Card description

Mercurius senex, the Saturnian old man, reclines while exhaling spiritus and anima as two angels. A raven of the nigredo perches on him. Old man, angels, and raven are enclosed within the protective egg, womb, or alembic. Outside, Sol radiates intense heat while Wind or Aeolus blows in opposition. Above appear the Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, and Saturn.
