Card three, Desire,

Grade: Puffer, keyword Desire.

The picture shows a sea goddess wearing a crown, emerging from the waves, born of the deep sea who pours milk from her paps. As a sea-goddess, she is a mermaid and has two large tails with which curl up behind her. Her hair hangs down in profusion, like a wealth of green seaweed.

The picture contains the elements of two and one. Two in the breasts, and the twin tails, and one in the sea-goddess figure. Desire—not to acquire, but to provide, is your strongest feeling and need at this time. Sharing what you have and what you can provide is important to you. Acknowledging others who have less, or who have nothing, is a prime importance. In giving to others, you will feel like a queen or king, indeed almost like a god or goddess. Yet there should be no ego in the giving. It should be done from the heart, with the true desire to help others.

You have great compassion, and are very generous, but beware of vanity, beware of the ego trip. Better to give anonymously than to announce to the world that you are giving. Indeed, you will find it far more satisfying to work this way. Always ask yourself why you do what you do. Secret knowledge that you can be the means to ease the suffering of others, will give you tremendous satisfaction. Contemplating this card can help you achieve a wonderful sense of purpose. The sea, and water in general, ties in strongly with spirituality.

Giving, sharing your spirituality is as important, perhaps even more so, as giving, as the giving of material objects, possessions, finances. Teaching and spreading your religious beliefs will not only benefit others, but will also give you the satisfaction of knowing, that you are being a catalyst for others' spiritual development. The color of the suit is blue and its light aspect, blue is the color of tranquility, patience, and understanding. The numbers one and two are significant in this picture. One is the number associated with the sun. Its color is gold, and is the color of the sea-goddess's crown.

One stands for individuality, rulership, and self-confidence. These are all things which you have. One is the number of leadership, and you have such qualities within you. It oughts also the number of the explorer, explore the many ways in which you can offer help to others Be aware of the onset of vanity. Try to avoid feelings that you know it all. Teaching others can lay you open to the ego trip. Give of yourself, but do so openly and freely, with no thought of recompense. Nurture the desire to give, and enjoy it. Two is the number of the moon, a number of balance and of union.

By recognizing a desire, and fulfilling it, you achieve the balance. You have a great inner strength and that will carry you forward, far beyond what you may see as your limits. Changes are inevitable, so learn to accept them. The two tails that rise behind the figure are symbols of strength, with the ability to drive forward the individual, in any direction desired. There's both power and flexibility. The two breasts are symbols of nurturing and of compassion. They are founts of nourishment, both material and spiritual.
