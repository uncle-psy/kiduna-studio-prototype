## Complete narrative

this card indicates that you are able to create. At any time that you are forced to remain still, your mind will continue to race, conjuring up ideas, plans, plots, and schemes for the future. You have a very active imagination, but you have so many ideas, you don't have time to mold them all, into workable overall scheme. The card says that you If you can make yourself concentrate on one line of thought, you'll be able to create something truly worthwhile. You have a sense of loss, or of being wounded in some way, but it is an illness you have suffered. Perhaps it is an illness you have suffered.

Have you reflected on death, and become uncertain of your feelings about it? This card shows a residual fear of what the afterlife holds, and yet there is no real reason to fear death. In the picture, a hand reaches down, to help and to aid, but it is nowhere near time for death, you have much more creating within you, and already there is an opus magnum, which you wish to achieve. It is growing within you, and trying to burst forth. Organize yourself, and your thoughts, and you'll be able to achieve wonders.

It is as though you were lying on a barren piece of land, yet are able to make the trees and flowers to bloom. The moon, as a deity figure, looks down, and watches over you. This indicates that there is an elderly father figure, who watches over you in this way, whether on this plane or from beyond. Here is someone who is, or was, near and dear to you. Create, and may come proud. The element of one, is ever present in this picture, with the one main figure, the one tree, the one hand, and the one moon. One is emphasized again, and again. It is the driving life force. It is ambition.

It is a leader, aligned with the sun, it is a vital principle, without which there would be no life. Find your direction, and create. There are great things ahead of you.

Planetary conjunction: Sun Venus. There is luck, and love for you. You are much admired, and sought after. You possess a deep inner attraction for others, yet there is a refinement to your nature, making you seem sensitive. Creative abilities are present, very strongly. You must ignore imagined or actual hurts and look to the future, for it is a bright future. There is help within reach, if, ever you should need it, and there is someone who is always watching over you, to help you up when you fall, and to encourage you in your endeavors.

Stone: jade. The green jade has for centuries, been associated with the occult wisdom. The possesses healing properties, and is said to ensure a peaceful death. Since it is said to stimulate wisdom, jade is often referred to as the philosopher's gemstone, and was greatly revered by the alchemists of old. The Spanish, in their conquest of Central, and South America, regarded jade as a cure for, and protection against kidney diseases. Varieties of it are called jadeite, and nephrite. Jade was placed in the mouths of corpses, of corpses, in Egypt, Mexico, and China.

In China, it was also sometimes used to cover the nose, mouth, eyes, and ears. It has been used in talismans around the world.

Grade: This card is, in the Adept grade

## Card description

a man, a supremum materia, pierced by the arrow of Mer-Mercurius. The arbor philosophica, the tree of the philosophers, or alchemists, grows out of him. A hand points down from a cloud, and the moon, as a man, observes the scene.
