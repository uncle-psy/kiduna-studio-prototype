## Card description

An alchemical laboratory contains three main processes. At the rear is a large glass alembic holding liquid, topped by an inverted distillation retort. To the left, an overflowing copper tub on a small athanor pours through a brass funnel into a glass alembic. The main two-tiered athanor burns with flames at its top; an upper liquid chamber carries a copper tube and inverted retort. A door opens outside at left and a barred window pierces the rear stone wall.
