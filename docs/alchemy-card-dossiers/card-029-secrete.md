## Complete narrative

To SECRETE is to hide or conceal. In the illustration it is the art of alchemy that must be secreted. But for you, at this time, it is something (or all) that is of importance to you. Do not confide in anyone and everyone. This card indicates that you should not share your thoughts, hopes, or desires at this time. If you have some invention, some idea that you want to develop, or some planned course of action, keep the details to yourself. There are those who would not necessarily be sympathetic to you or your beliefs. Purple is the color of this suit.

Purple is associated with ambition, power, tension, and business progress. Mercury is associated with the intellect; with the workings of the brain. Here it is bottled up, and carefully watched. The tongs indicate that it must be handled carefully. The sieves suggest that any information that is given out must be carefully reviewed and sifted before distribution. The number Two is the number of the Moon. The two points of the crescent moon are sharp while the rest of the shape is rounded, showing a dichotomy. There are things that need to be spread yet they must be kept secret.

There is a spark of creativity but a general passivity.

Planetary conjunction: MOON-URANUS There is a high level of emotional tension here. You are generally very sharing and it goes against the grain to be close-mouthed. There is often a tendency to ignore advice, but that should not be the case at this time. You feel a strong desire to be independent right now. An interest in New Age topics is good, since much of the philosophy will help you put things into perspective.

Stone: OPAL This is a jewel of the water yet it possesses tremendous fire energy. Some consider it a very lucky stone while others have the reverse opinion. It seems that it either appeals to you or it does not. Yet there are he 113 The Cards times when you should wear or carry an opal, even if it does not draw you. Now is one of those times, for the protective aura of the opal is right for you at present. It is a soft energy yet a vibrant protection Itisa stone of hope and achievement. It amplites the chakras and enhances your intuition.

Grade: This card is in the Puffer grade—you are the apprentice, with much to learn. In all that has been said, above, remember that you are at the beginning and have a long road to travel. Pace yourself and, especially, learn from your experiences.

## Card description

An alchemist and his wife or female alchemist stand among alchemical tools. Each presses one finger to the lips and raises a finger of the other hand, signaling silence. Between them is a glass retort holding the sign of Mercury or the Philosopher's Stone, with tongs beneath it. Above each head is a pan or mesh screen receiving material poured from a copper cup.
