## Complete narrative

Master is the keyword. This card indicates that by nature, you are a leader. You have it within yourself to master all challenges placed before you. You have a knack for delegating duties, for having others work with you. You have healing abilities, which you should draw upon. You tend to look for challenges, be it a simple crossword puzzle, or the complexity of an automobile's wiring. You grow restless when there is nothing to occupy you. There is special strength that may be gathered from your feminine side, the strength of patience and understanding, of meditation, consideration, and diplomacy.

Now may be a time to celebrate some achievements. If so, then spoil yourself. You deserve it. Acknowledge any help and assistance you receive. As a natural leader, people tend to focus on you, but never hesitate to see that credit goes where credit is due. You have a powerful guardian angel, higher self, or spirit guide who watches over and guides you. Do not neglect your spiritual side. If problems are encountered in any other cards, drawing this Wild Card indicates that you will master those problems.

Whatever happens to you, no matter how dire it may seem, you will have the ability to rise from the ashes and start over. Ending means new beginnings. Rebirth opens you up to a whole new field of opportunities. Fire element, seen in both creator and destroyer, regarded as humankind's greatest invention, fire transforms as noted. Ending brings new beginnings. It is renewal, a rebirth. So does the Phoenix rise from the flames. Fire is powerful but must be controlled. You have the power to control it. Use that power wisely. Fire is cleansing, fire is energizing, fire is sexual energy.

The universal evidence of wood friction. As the earliest means of starting fire, in reference to it and myth and folklore, would seem to point to it as the original method. You have the power to master people and situations by rubbing together elements or personalities, to create fire, leading to energy and change. Mercury correspondence:. Mercury is associated with the brain and the nervous system, with the intellect, with mental perception. It gives the urge to acquire knowledge and to share that knowledge with others.

It shows excellent reasoning powers, perceptiveness, and versatility, but it can also make you argumentative, sarcastic, and cynical. Many of these positive traits, and even the negative ones, can be used for good if handled wisely.
