## Complete narrative

This card indicates you are ready to shoulder the responsibilities that come with your position. Circumstances are such that you may use your influence to aid others. You may or may not be aware of injustices. With little effort, you can discover such examples and work to correct them. Set yourself the task of performing this service. You will find that you will grow immeasurably in your own mind. Your self-esteem will be excellent. The color purple is a royal color, indicating power, ambition, and business progress. It is closely allied to the number one, which is the active driving number of the sun.

Yet here, the one is aligned with the moon. Since the Sun is a masculine energy and the Moon a feminine one, the result is a perfect balance, the perfect couple.

Planetary conjunction: Moon, Pluto. Their experiences that will help you move above the emotional attachments of this world. You are highly creative and very active. Although you may experience some of the emotional extremes, you will grow stronger from those experiences. At this time, there's a feeling of need to distance yourself from family. You must resist any impulsiveness, however. Beware of jealousy developing in any form. There are new situations developing all the time. Try to flow with them rather than flight-fight against them.

Stone: amethyst. The amethyst is a variety of quartz, a semi-precious stone found in varying shades of purple. It owes its color to oxide of manganese and iron found in its composition. It is an excellent aid for meditation and for healing. It is said to dispel illusion and to promote psychic ability, especially the ability to channel. It is a protective stone and regarded as the stone of the high priest. For centuries, it has been recognized as an antidote to inebriation, and people would drink water from a glass containing amethyst stones, to cure drunkenness.

It’d be beneficial to you to carry a piece of amethyst at all times, either a piece of the stone or a piece of jewelry containing the stone. It is said to confer the vision of love on its owner. This card is in the puffer grade

Luna stands within the crescent Moon with one hand slightly raised, dressed in purple robes. She is the single central figure and protective presence.
