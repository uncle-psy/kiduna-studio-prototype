## Complete narrative

finalize is the keyword. There are three numbers of significance apparent in the picture, one, two, and three. One is a number of the tempter. One is a powerful number, a leader, a driving force, tending to be impatient. He, she, frequently assumes the big brother sister role. It is easy to give in to the influence of this figure. Two is the number of balance and union. Two can repel as well as attract. In this instance, however, the two is composed of male and female, a perfect balance. Yet there is also the triad, the two, plus the one. There is a number of, f-foundation.

It is the number associated with Jupiter, the planet aligned with Venus in this card, is a number of trusting, perhaps of the gullible. There is, therefore, a need for caution, yet a need to finalize the situation, in order to go on, to build a strong foundation. Two is also represented by the gold jug and the goblet. There is a connection with sensitivity and even with tears. Once again, caution should be exercised, yet the time has come to finalize the situation.

The temptation is to lose yourself in the attractiveness of the city, to drink deeply to the cup and to savor the pleasures of the moment, while delaying the inevitable consequences of your action. Finalize.

Planetary conjunction: Venus, Jupiter. This conjunction shows a popularity that stems from an abundance of feelings. Don't let yourself, don't let your feelings carry you away. Try to keep things in perspective. Partnership of all kinds can be very beneficial at this time, but there's a tremendous sense of drama, together with cravings and tendencies toward excess. Avoid unnecessary extravagance and control your appetite for all things.

Stone: Emerald. Much use in rituals in love. In ancient Egypt, this stone was also connected with resurrection. The third party here, may or may not be an actual person. It could be another situation or position, which is a temptation. The card's indicate, that it is time to examine all the ramifications of the situation, and to come to a decision. You're in some ways very naive, and must acknowledge that, but you are no longer a true innocent.

You must draw on your own experiences, and the experience of others, in order to face up to the situation, and arrive at a decision. You must then act on that decision. Remember, finalize is the keyword.
