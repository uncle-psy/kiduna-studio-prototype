The Cards realize jade 9: REALIZE

Keyword: Realize. Gifts: Inspiration. Insight. Wisdom. Challenges: Achieving balance. Patience. The illustration is from the Mutus liber in quo tamen tota Philosophia hermetica, figuris hieroglyphicis depingitur (La Rochelle, 1677).

The picture shows Mercurius in the "philosopher's egg" (the alchemical vessel), which is held by two angels, one draped in green and the other in blue. Regarded as a chthonic deity of revelation, and also the spirit of quicksilver, Mercurius is shown standing on the Sun and the Moon, tokens of his dual nature. He is, in fact, the product of the marriage between Sol and Luna. He is closest to the Sun as the planet Mercury, so is especially related to the gold. Yet as quicksilver, he dissolves I34 Egg Suit the gold, thus eliminating its sun-like brilliance.

The Sun's rays produce the heat to ripen the homunculus in the vessel. The ten birds correspond to the serpents of the caduceus. They also represent spiritualization. Two of the birds carry branches bearing the symbols of solar and lunar essences already obtained. The elements of One, Two, Three, and Ten are present. One is the Sun, Iwo is the pair of angels, Three is the angels together with Mercurius, and Ten is the flock of birds. REALIZE that you are in the process of change, with many people and many elements at work to bring this about.

You are the result of the joining of two major factors and so should be well balanced and adjusted. You hold in your hand the power to do a lot of good for others. In the future you will be in a position to do much healing. There is also travel in your future. You are watched over by two strong guardians; aspects of the Higher Self. A strong spirituality is present, making a connection between you and some form of religion, be it traditional or nontraditional. You could be a driving force in this religion or spiritualistic movement.

Fortune smiles on you but at times you may feel that the spotlight is a little too harsh. There will be times when you will yearn for the shade; for some respite from playing in the center of the stage. Yet you do have a part to play, and an important one. There are constant messages coming and going. There is a lot of communication concerning you at this time. One is the Sun, the major force that looks down on you and influences you. It is all-seeing, leaving little room for you to make a mistake. Iwo is Luna, the Moon. Here is tact and diplomacy. There is also sensitivity, and wisdom beyond your years.

Three is the number of the joygiver. It is creative talent, artistic feeling, and self-expression. Its negative aspects include a need to develop patience, for you want to get things done right away. Also, do not indulge in gossip. Ten is a higher aspect of One, indicating a bright future with much hard, but rewarding, work. Green is the color of healing, also of luck and fertility. Stone: JADE The green jade has, for centuries, been associated with occult wisdom. It possesses healing properties and is said to ensure a peaceful death. Since it is said to stimulate wisdom, jade is often referred to as the philosopher's gemstone and was greatly revered by the alchemists of old. The Spanish, in their conquest of Central and South America, regarded jade as a cure for, and protection against, kidney diseases. Varieties of it are called jadeite and nephrite. Jade was placed in the mouths of corpses in Egypt, Mexico, and China.

In China it was also sometimes used to cover the nose, mouth, eyes, and ears. It has been much used in talismans around the world.

## Card description

Mercurius stands inside the philosopher's egg, held by two angels—one draped in green and the other in blue. Mercurius stands on the Sun and Moon as tokens of dual nature. The Sun shines above. Ten birds represent spiritualization and correspond to the serpents of the caduceus; two carry branches bearing solar and lunar essences.
