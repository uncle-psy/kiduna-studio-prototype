## Complete narrative

The illustration, no,

The picture shows the mercurial-like figure of Spiritus Sulphur standing, as though in contemplation, amidst the cre-the clouds. He holds a thorough ball of smoking sulfur. A green cloth is held, falling across the figure. The distillation of sulfur was important to the alchemical process, yet it is not certain what the practitioners meant by sulfur. It seems unlikely that it was a solid substance known by the name today, since it that has a boiling point of 440 degrees Celsius. Also, its vapor condenses, condenses to a liquid that defies, a liquid that solidifies on cooling.

The product of the alchemist distillation procedure, was known as hudor, meaning sulfurous water, or divine water. But the descriptions of the process suggest a liquid, rather than a solid used in the practice. Indications are that it was actually eggs that were used, breaking down to a clear alkaline liquid, a yellowish distillate, containing ammonium sulfide, and a thick, dark, yellowish liquid.

The picture contains the element of one. There is a need to complete what you have started. Indeed, there may be a number of things, which you have begun, but have not seen thought, through to finish. You have great leadership potential, but you need to be an example to others by covering all aspects of the subject. If you would be a role model for others, you must, be so by following through on every project from start to finish. There is no time to stand, and preen, to wait for admiration. You do not hesitate to undertake anything, even seemingly foolhardy endeavors.

But many but many of the things you undertake, you do so, for the effect it will create. You can be too theatrical. Back to the card. Part of the problem is that you can be in too much of a hurry, wanting to rush through and get things done. It pays to take the necessary time to complete a project. There's a difference between doing a thing quickly, and doing it too quickly. There's also applies to judgment, you can be quick to judge, both people, and things. In order to really complete a thing, you need to do the thing thoroughly. The element of one, is the number associated with the sun. Its color is gold.

One stands for individuality, rulership, leadership, and confidence. These are all things which you have. It is also the number of the explorer. Explore the many ways in which you can offer help to others, but beware of the onset of vanity. Try to avoid feelings that you know it all. Teaching others can lay you open to the ego trip. Give of yourself, but do Give of yourself, but do so openly, and freely, with no thought of recompense. Nurture the desire to give, and enjoy it. Above all, take the time to do things thoroughly, and completely. Green, the color of the cloth held, is the color of the earth.

It is also the color of healing, and of fertility. There is an association with money, with finance. As it is held in the picture, this is not one of the greatest importance to you. Yet, It is there, if you need it.

Planetary conjunction: Sun, Moon, a powerful combination, and a well balanced one. You have the best of male and female energies. You express what you feel. But you can be blunt, sometimes to the point of rudeness. You need, enjoy an audience for success. Beware of arrogance, and of being overbearing.

Stone: turquoise. A stone once used in rituals, to incite, love and passion. Turquoise is closely linked to Venus. It helps transit energy from one to another, and is a stone that aids communication. It also brings peace of mind, promotes loyalty, and has a tremendous calming influence.

Grade: this card is the Adept grade
