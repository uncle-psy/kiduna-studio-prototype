## Card description

a hermaphrodite, the male side of which is holding the male sun, and the female side of which is holding the feminine moon. It symbolizes the bringing together of these two elements, the harmony of true balance of energy. The second illustration is the twin headed or hermaphroditic Melusina, alchemy often represented Mercurius as virgin above and serpent below.
