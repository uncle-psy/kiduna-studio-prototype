## Complete narrative

Execute is the keyword. It is the time to put plans into action, take charge and move forward, examine your current situation, weigh up the other card drawn with this wild card. Know that you have the elements of this card in reserve to use with or against the forces displayed in your other card, and execute accordingly. Fear must be slain, and you must face up to responsibilities. This card represents raw earth at your command, ready for you to execute. It is your trump card, literally, and that you have both lion energy and dragon energy at your call if you should ever need it.

Here's a chance to draw forth necessary power and strength. Here is a reservoir of opportunity. Use this power wisely and only when considered necessary. Take the time to sit and study this card, drawing on it for insight and inspiration. Let it help you develop your responsibilities and help you achieve a greater sense of self-worth and pride. Earth element, You're practical and stable, like the dragon, you have your feet planted firmly on the ground. It is from the earth, from the ground, you can most easily draw your power.

You can be tolerant, loyal, and diplomatic, but if riled, you can make a dangerous enemy. Deep within, you have the courage of the lion. Mercury correspondence:. Mercury is associated with the brain and the nervous system, with the intellect, with mental perception. It gives the urge to acquire knowledge and to share that knowledge with others. It shows excellent reasoning powers, perceptiveness and versatility, but can also make you argumentative, sarcastic, and cynical. Many of these positive traits and even the negative ones, can be used for good if handled wisely

## Card description

The key illustration is a great green lion with a Sun between its jaws and blood falling to the ground. The lion may be disgorging rather than devouring the blood and Sun. The secondary illustration is a dragon standing in a field near a tree stump, apparently at rest but alert.
