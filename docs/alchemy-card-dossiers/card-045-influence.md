## Complete narrative

This card indicates that you have it in you to be a great teacher, and therefore to influence others. Obtain a good grounding in your subject. Try to know everything about it before you try to teach it. If you are asked a question and you don't know the answer, admit it. Don't be afraid to confess, confess that you don't know something. But then make it your job to find the answer. One is the number of the individualist. You have creative thoughts and inventive ideas. You have courage and initiative, originality and self-determination. Two is the number of balance and union. But two can repel as well as attract.

In this instance however, the two is composed of male and female, a perfect balance. There's arbitration and agreement. Don't spend too much time and get lost in small details. Three is the number of imagination and inspiration. It is also a number of emotion. In its negative aspect, it is a number of selfishness and extravagance. Beware of these properties. Four is a practical number, the number of order and stability. It is the number of individualists seemingly not greatly concerned with material matters, who are not easily swayed by money. They may however be very good at handling finances.

It indicates that there can be sudden unexpected feelings of loneliness and isolation, which in turn can lead to despondency and melancholy. You may have a hard time making friends at this time, but those friends are fiercely loyal when you do so.

Planetary conjunction: Mars, Moon. This is a powerful combination giving authority without causing resentment. At this time you are well able to deal with both individuals and with large groups of people. But beware developing a short temper. Much like the moon and the sun working together, the moon and Mars gives a nice balance of male and female energies. Try to avoid recklessness and ignoring future results of present actions. Look ahead and try to judge where your actions will lead.

Stone: black tourmaline. This is a stone of imaginati… …s, Moon.

This is a stone of imagination and intuition. It is also a stone of protection. It will help you overcome fear. The tourmaline can become electrically charged by rubbing it. One end becomes positive and the other negative. It is therefore a stone of attraction and will draw to you that which you desire.

Grade: this card is in the Adept grade

## Card description

the squaring of the circle, to make two sexes one whole, a mage or alchemist stands to one side manipulating a huge compass. On a wall that is the side of a building, is inscribing a circle. Within that circle is found a triangle containing a square, within which is a circle enclosing a man and a woman. The alchemist plans and drafting implements lay on the ground beside him. The triangle represents body, mind, and soul. The square is the four elements of earth, air, fire, and water. The circle is the transformation itself, showing the need for both male and female as a balance of energies.
