## Complete narrative

Challenges. Detachment. Direction. Pride. Romanticism.

The picture shows a snake that has been crucified. It hangs from a rough wooden cross, suspended by two nails. At the foot of the cross lies a heavily bound open book. The cross is on a low hill, not far from a church or similar building. The setting is obviously rural, with no sign of houses.

The picture contains the element of one. The keyword is restore, and may well apply to restoring faith in yourself. You've made mistakes and, through one of these, or simply through circumstances, you find yourself in a position of suspense. The prospects look bleak, and there seems to be no immediate way out. You may ask yourself how come you came to be in this situation. Look back on your past actions and analyze what has happened. Rather than blaming yourself or others for the situation, look upon everything as a lesson that needs to be learned. The main question is not how. You came to be in this situation.

It is how you're going to get out of it. From the building in the background, it might be that religious or spiritual values, Possibly from the distant past, have brought you to the present situation. But at your feet. As it were, lies a book of knowledge. Once you can extricate yourself from the immediate restrictions, you will have access to it. It is through knowledge and learning that you will progress. Snakes are able to shed their skin, and it is through this process that you maybe able to escape, The hangup you find occurring right now. By leaving the old and accepting the new.

By making plans for a whole new approach to life, You may well be able to slough off your present situation, and move onward. One of your weaknesses is a lack of direction. In your present forced inactivity, Take the time to plan your future. Look at where you want to be a year from now, five years, ten years, and so on. Make long term plans, and know that you'll stick to them. Once you're again on the move. The color of this suit is brown. This is the color of uncertainty. Take uncertainty as a challenge, and change it to certainty. The element of one is predominant.

This in This is the number of the sun, the center of the solar system. All the planets revolve around it, as the snake is able to revolve around a central point. One stands for individuality, But in the present situation, you may not be able to afford to be an individual for a while. You may help, you may need to call on others to help you. Don't be too proud to do this. You have organizing ability. Use it. Astronaut-logical balance, Sun Neptune. Sensitivity is the word for this conjunction. As an individualist, You prefer to remain aloof, yet the present circumstances call for you to turn to others for aid.

You do prefer to live somewhere secluded, and you are a very creative person. Don't let your life be ruled by romantic notions. It is necessary to be practical to survive in today's world. Beware of drugs of any sort. Despite a fascination they have for you, they would not do you any good at all.

Stone: lodestone. The lodestone, magnetite, has been regarded as magical for centuries, because of its magnetic properties.
