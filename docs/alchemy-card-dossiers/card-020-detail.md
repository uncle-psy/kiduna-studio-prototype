Card two, detail,

Grade: Puffer, keyword detail, gifts, analytical mind, good memory, teaching skills. The illustration is from Abu-Jabir. I don't need to read that.

The picture shows two alchemists working to prepare what will be placed, in a large furnace between them. The outer brick furnace contains a copper container in which sits an iron container. These various vessels, including the main furnace, are all open, and ready to receive the items.

The picture contains the elements of two, and three. The two alchemists, and the three containers. Detail work is an important part of alchemy. Every little ingredient has to be carefully measured, and weighed. Preparation is dependent upon many things, including temperature, time, and placement. Your own work is equally demanding of attention to detail. Your work, and even your whole life, can benefit from this attention. Without it, things can go wrong. Small things can work a domino effect, and gradually build into larger problems. You must therefore, pay close attention to detail.

What may seem small, and insignificant may in fact be an essen-an-essential ingredient, that affects the whole. Start taking a little more care, with what you do. Try to keep the overall picture in mind, the desired end result. There are open vessels about you at the moment. These may be, inanimate, or animate, co-workers perhaps, or family members. There are many different varieties. You are the one who will be filling these vessels. You may not feel that you play a very important part, but in fact you do. You have good teaching skills, and are able to pass along knowledge in an effective manner.

But beware of becoming too aggressive in what you do, and say. Two is a main element of this picture, and two is the number of the moon. It shines with reflective light. As it crescent, it has the twin horns that may spur upon others. But when in its full glory, gives light, encouragement, to all. But when in its full glory, it gives light, and encouragement to all, if in, a passive manner. Three is the number of Mercury, so important to the alchemical process. Mercury is the messenger, just as you can be the messenger in passing on knowledge. It is a number of talent and enthusiasm.

Planetary conjunction: Jupiter, Uranus. There is a resistance to restrictions, and a great desire for independence.

There is resistance to restrictions, and a great desire for independence. Leadership is apparent, but there can be a tendency toward unnecessary aggressiveness. Conventionality is not one of your strong points, with a predisposition to be eccentric to some degree.

Stone: is ruby. The ruby is so named because of its red color, from the Latin, rubeus. It is a stone of integrity. It is also the stone, that was thought to reflect the spark of life. It is a stone of regeneration, of protection. And it promotes good health. It was once believed to lay a ruby, on a wound would stop the bleeding.

Grade: this card is in the Puffer grade. You are the apprentice, with much to learn. In all that has been said above, remember that are at the beginning and have a long road to travel. Pace yourself, and especially, learn from your experiences
