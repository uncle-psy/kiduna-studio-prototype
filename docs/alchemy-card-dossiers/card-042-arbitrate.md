## Complete narrative

Athanor Suit e arbitrate sapphire 6: ARBITRATE

Keyword: Arbitrate. Gifts: Diplomacy. Understanding. Challenges: Egotism. The illustration is from D. Stolcius von Stolcenberg's Viridarium chymicum (Frankfort, 1624). The picture has been described as symbolizing a purifying bath of two conflicting materials, shown as two fighters. The one on the right, with the eagle on his sword, is sal ammoniac; the one on the left is saltpeter. The philosophical Mercury in the middle symbolizes the distillate of the two. There are elements in the picture of Garudas fight against the Nagas, in Hindu mythology.

Winged and crowned Mercury stands with a caduceus raised in each hand. The symbol for mercury is above his head. The Sun is on one side 155 The Cards of him and the Moon on the other. Beneath him are spread wings. He stands between two combatants. The one on the left holds a weapon around which a crowned snake is entwined. The one on the opposite side holds a sword, on the tip of which sits a golden eagle. The elements of One, Iwo, and Three are in the picture. This card places you in a strong position to ARBITRATE. The caduceus is the healing staff.

In tantra, the two snakes are wound about a lingam, or phallus, representing the kundalini power. The caduceus is the sign generally acknowledged to represent the healer. Here you are in possession of great healing powers (with two caduceus staffs), and therefore in a position to do much good. The crown indicates that you are in a position of authority; the nakedness shows innocence, or complete neutrality. You place yourself between two combatants. This card shows that you go out of your way to stop those with disagreements that have got, or are getting, out of hand.

The fully spread wings of Justice lay at your feet; the Sun and the Moon are beside you. You are in a strong position to arbitrate because of the great respect in which you are held. People recognize your judgment as always being fair. It is said that Mercury was the child of the Sun and the Moon and, as such, has both male and female elements. Sometimes he/she may be depicted as a male spirit within a female body and sometimes, as here, as a female spirit within a male body. You have strong affinity with both sexes and this is one of the reasons you can be such a good arbitrator.

One is seen in the figure of Mercury and also in the set of wings. Iwo is in the Sun and the Moon, the two caduceus staffs, and the two combatants. Three is the two combatants together with Mercury. One, the number associated with the Sun (though here a Sun/Moon combination) stands for individuality and self-confidence together with diplomacy and tact. The spread wings emphasize creative thought. These are all things which you have.

But try to avoid feelings that you "know it all" Take the time to do things thoroughly and completely. 156 Athanor Suit Two is here the balance of male and female energies; the yin-yang of life. The Sun is assertion and rulership, whilst the Moon is receptivity and personality. The combatants bring in the possibility of strife and discord but that is diluted by the two caduceus staffs. Three is a number of foundation. It is the basis of the tripod on which sat the Oracle at Delphi. It has for millennia been regarded as a "magical" number.

In numerology, this number indicates a tendency to rise to positions of authority, seemingly with little effort. You could well excel in positions of trust and authority. However, beware of becoming dictatorial. You could make enemies without realizing it.

Planetary conjunction: MARS-SUN The Sun and Mars are two powerful planets. Together they give tremendous energy and ambition to you. There is a need for constant release of that energy. There is a feeling of power through action. You have great faith in your own abilities but you must become more aware of your limitations, for you cannot always win. You must learn to exercise selfcontrol.

Stone: SAPPHIRE The blue sapphire, whose color ranges from very light to dark royal blue, is considered a stone of spiritual enlightenment in Buddhism. The star sapphire was attributed to Saturn, most ancient of the gods, but has also been ascribed to Mercury. In many places it is regarded as the stone of wisdom. It is a stone of good omen, bringing health, strength, money, and favors from authority figures. Necromancers believed that a sapphire amulet or talisman would enable them to understand convoluted oracles from the dead. It was long used as a protection against the evil eye and other negative magic. 157

## Card description

Winged and crowned Mercury stands neutrally between two combatants with one caduceus raised in each hand and the Mercury sign above. The Sun is on one side and the Moon on the other; spread wings lie beneath. The left fighter holds a weapon entwined by a crowned snake, while the right fighter holds a sword tipped by a golden eagle.
