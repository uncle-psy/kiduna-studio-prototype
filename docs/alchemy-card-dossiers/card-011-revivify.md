## Complete narrative

Two, Revivify.

The picture shows a royal couple lying on a tomb. Sorry.

The picture shows a royal couple lying in a tomb. On one legged man stands pointing at them, as though making a statement. Death, in the form of a scythe holding, a scythe holding skeleton, extends an arm in their direction. Behind the scene flows a river. On the far side of which is a country town or city.

The picture contains the elements of one, two, and four. There to be a very strong message here about conflict. As depicted in this picture, a one-legged man wants to revive the royal couple, and return them from death. Here he is pleading their cause, while the death figure is seemingly proud of his work, of placing them in the tomb. To revivify, is to restore, to bring back to life, to make something active, and to flourish again. This card is an indicator that you are in a position to do just that. The couple is crowned, signifying some substance or power.

They are people of property and influence, although seemingly dead, they are in a state of limbo where their fate can still be bargained over. The outcome is not yet final. A person who has made some great personal sacrifices, in the past, is trying to revive them, trying to replace them to their former glory. Yet another person of incredible power, who has been responsible for disposal of many others, is gloatingly making a statement to the effect, that what has been done cannot be undone. All around the couple are signs of life, behind them runs a river. Its water signifying is spiritual passage.

Flowers and plants spring up from the fertile ground. Beyond the river is a city, possibly the city over which the couple ruled. Its fate, too, must hang in the balance. Which of these figures are you? The outcome is very important. Why is that? In what way will the outcome affect you, and affect others? Beware of vanity. Be it from the power that has brought you here or the influence that will bring you back. There is a conflict that has been building for some time. Now it is coming to a head. Consider all ramifications before arriving at a decision.

There are three numbers of significance, apparent in the picture, two, three, and four. The two of the couple in the tomb, the three of the couple plus the individual pleading their cause, and the four of all the figures in the picture. Two is the number of balance and union. In this instance, the two is composed of male and female, a perfect balance. Yet the balance is held in abeyance for a moment. Three is a number of foundation. It is the basis of the tripod. On which sat the oracle at Delphi. It is four millennia. It has for millennia, been regarded as a magical number.

In numerology, this number indicates a tendency to rise, to positions of authority, seemingly with little effort. Yet here the rise would seem to have been made through the sacrifice, apparently willing, of a third party. In numerology, the number four is a practical number, the number of order and stability. Can you bring order out of chaos? Four indicates that there can be sudden, unexpected feelings of loneliness and isolation, which in turn can lead to despondency and melancholy. You may have a hard time making friends at this time, but when you do so, those friends are fiercely loyal.

Planetary conjunction: Sun, Uranus. These two signs in conjunction, indicate a certain amount of stubbornness. Having made a decision, you are loath to change it, even when you realize a change could be for the better. You can be vain, and you can be deceitful if you think it will serve you. There are great powers of leadership evident, but there is also a lack of diplomacy. There is also a sense of the dramatic, often played, even when not necessary. Beware of eccentricity to the extreme. Don't try force your values, especially religious ones, on others.

Stone: amber, soothing, harmonizing, a… …lourish again.

Stone: amber, soothing, harmonizing, awakens the Kundalini. Amber has been treasured since earliest times. The Greeks called it the tears of the sisters of Phaethon, those who were turned into poplar trees. Amber necklaces have long been favored by witches. Times the amber being imbalanced with jet. It was for many years used to make the mouthpieces of cigarette holders, due to the belief that it prevented the transmission of infectious diseases. The Chinese use it in incense. Grade. There are powerful forces at work with this card, yet it is in the puffer grade.

Try to remember that you are the apprentice, with still much to learn
