# Philosophy Power Map v0.1

Status: production-complete review release; not canonically registered or published.

This release contains 54 philosophy Cards connected to the 54-node interactive map. Each Card is a wordless visual argument: one governing mechanism, one dominant image, and one material frame. The three approved visual families are the Immanent Orchard, the Cartesian Theater, and the Infinite Gift Shop.

## Contents

- `cards/raw/`: preserved image-generation masters.
- `cards/finished/`: 1024 × 1536 RGBA Cards with transparent exteriors and opaque interiors.
- `qa/contact-sheets/`: six visual-review sheets covering every Card.
- `card-manifest.json`: Card-to-node mapping, semantic kernel, family, paths, checksum, and dimensional ranges.
- `graph.json`: 54 nodes and 61 relationships used by the interactive map.
- `coherence.json`: the editorial dimensions and the declared map/node valence model.
- `validation-report.json`: coverage and technical-QA summary.
- `PRODUCTION-BRIEF.md`: visual grammar and Card-by-Card mechanisms.

## Review decisions

- Spinoza, Dennett, and Derrida are the approved source samples that establish the three families.
- All remaining Cards inherit those approved grammars but remain individually replaceable without changing node identity.
- “Keep,” “Repair,” and “Quarantine” are editorial provocations, not truth scores.
- The map is intentionally revision-friendly: no philosopher’s fame grants immunity, and no current scientific theory receives coronation.

## Technical contract

All finished Cards are PNG, 1024 × 1536, text-free, with a real alpha channel, fully transparent exterior pixels, antialiased boundaries, and fully opaque interiors. The raw source is preserved beside every finished Card.
