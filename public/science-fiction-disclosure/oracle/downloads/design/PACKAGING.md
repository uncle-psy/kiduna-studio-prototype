# Packaging

## Purpose

Packaging keeps the 480-Tile system usable as an archive, divination tool, and game kit rather than an undifferentiated collection.

## Invariants

- Organize the core into thirteen Class folios inside one archival case.
- Include an epistemic key, graph legend, source guide, spreads, game sheets, safety statement, and blank research slips.
- Physical and digital contents carry the same release number and checksum manifest.
- Packaging may summarize but may not hide evidence or safety boundaries.

## Production Rules

- Size folios for sleeved Tiles and include tactile or textual Class labels independent of color.
- Provide a complete inventory and replacement-file path.
- Digital exports include cards, graph, sources, schemas, manuals, accessibility text, and validation report.
- Use archival-safe materials and document print tolerances.

## States, Scale, and Accessibility

- Meaning and class remain legible at 96, 256, 512, 1024, and 2048 pixels.
- Selected, hover, focus, disabled, and high-attention states add interface contours outside the master art; they never destructively recolor the source.
- No state, class, claim, or evidence condition depends on color alone; accessible live text supplies title, class, evidence, and safety information.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] All 480 Tiles reconcile with manifest and folios.
- [ ] Class labels are accessible without color.
- [ ] Rules and safety sheet are present.
- [ ] Digital checksums reproduce.
- [ ] Vendor, materials, rights, and batch information are recorded.

## Rights and Provenance

Every asset must be original or used under documented permission. Record card ID, prompt or source file, creator/tool, model version where relevant, date, dimensions, color profile, source references, rights status, and checksum. Franchise-derived entries use critical symbolic abstraction and may not reproduce protected logos, characters, costumes, frames, screenshots, or production art. Sacred and community-held imagery requires context and authority; do not turn it into generic decoration.
