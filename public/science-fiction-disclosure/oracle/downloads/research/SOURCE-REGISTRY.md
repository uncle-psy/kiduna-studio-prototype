# Source Registry

## Use Contract

Sources are organized into five tiers: Tier 1 primary, official, archival, standard, or peer-reviewed; Tier 2 scholarly or strong institutional secondary; Tier 3 reputable synthesis; Tier 4 community or proponent primary testimony; Tier 5 discovery-only or original editorial synthesis. Tier measures source role, not whether every claim in a source is true.

A **concept-specific source** supports a bounded history, definition, community account, or empirical claim for that Tile. A **class-level contextual source** situates a Tile in a wider field but does not establish that Tile’s literal claims and does not automatically create a graph relationship. A sourced edge may cite only IDs registered on both endpoint cards, and only when that source materially supports the stated relation. Original divinatory text cites original-synthesis and never impersonates outside authority.

Editors must record publisher, date, type, scope, stable URL, access date, rights note, and the exact claim or context supported. Official agencies are sources for their own records and definitions, not automatically neutral arbiters of every contested interpretation. Community testimony documents community meaning, not scientific consensus. Quotes and embedded media require separate rights review.

## Maintenance

At each release, test URLs, preserve archived copies where rights allow, flag changed or withdrawn sources, and never silently replace a contradictory record. Add a new source ID rather than changing the identity of an old citation. Re-run endpoint intersection and source-coverage audits after every registry change.
