## Audit Method

For each release, compare actual Class counts with targets; Domain and Mode distribution; concept-specific source coverage; safety flags; edge degree and family diversity; cross-Domain bridges; lineage completeness; and visual confusion at thumbnail scale. Investigate clusters that rely on one source, one community voice, one era, or one geography. Coverage means meaningful differentiated treatment, not merely title presence.

## Open Work

Prioritize concept-specific sourcing for context-only cards, primary/community sources for living traditions, non-Western science-fiction histories, disability and care perspectives, and source renewal for current movements. New coverage must not be achieved by flattening culturally distinct ideas into generic analogues.
