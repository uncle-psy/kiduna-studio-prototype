# Starseeds

## Purpose

Starseed identities are documented as living spiritual cosmologies. The Oracle preserves their internal distinctions while rejecting racial hierarchy, inherited superiority, or factual certainty unsupported by evidence. This chapter is an index and a method: it shows how to explore the subject without collapsing documented history, current research, reported experience, fiction, community belief, and Oracle synthesis into one truth claim.

## Reading Method

1. **Locate the Tile.** Read its Primary Class to learn what kind of thing it is, then its Inquiry Domains to learn which questions it can illuminate.
2. **Name the material mode.** A science-fiction, mythology, political-mythology, experiential-report, or established-science label identifies context; it is not a score of spiritual or personal importance.
3. **Read claims layer by layer.** Begin with the literal-and-empirical layer, then the cultural-and-historical layer, and only then the symbolic-and-divinatory layer. An evidence state applies to one bounded claim, not to the Tile as a whole.
4. **Open the sources.** A concept-specific citation can support a history or bounded claim. A class-level contextual citation only locates the Tile in a wider field. Neither one certifies every interpretation.
5. **Follow typed edges.** Compare the exact verb and rationale. “Fictionalizes,” “opposes,” “echoes,” and “investigated-by” are not interchangeable. A resemblance edge is not proof of historical influence.
6. **Integrate through action.** Use Gift, Shadow, Threshold, and the integration practice to identify one grounded choice that remains useful even if a favored ontology is wrong.

1. **Synchronicity** is a Forces Tile centered on Civilization & Ecology. Its literal layer is **not-applicable**; its symbolic question is: “What is the smallest reversible action that could clarify meaningful coincidence placed within civilization & ecology? Answer by testing how meaningful coincidence placed within civilization & ecology changes the conditions under which every actor chooses against shared survival, place, stewardship, access, feedback, and unequal exposure to risk.”
2. **Gnostic Cosmos** is a Cosmologies Tile centered on Reality & Ontology. Its literal layer is **not-applicable**; its symbolic question is: “Which authority governs without being ultimate?”
3. **Akashic Cosmos** is a Cosmologies Tile centered on Reality & Ontology. Its literal layer is **not-applicable**; its symbolic question is: “What are you trying to remember, and what source can ground it?”

Compare these examples by asking what changes when the class, evidence state, or edge verb changes. Do not treat the most vivid Tile as the most factual. When a reading crosses into health, identity, conspiracy, or current politics, show the card’s safety flags and preserve an alternate explanation.
