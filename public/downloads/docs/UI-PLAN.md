# Complete UI Plan

## Product outcome

Studio should make a large relational world feel understandable from the Source's current position. A Source sees where they are, what surrounds them, who or what is present, why an action is available, and what will happen before committing to it.

The Field remains continuous. Moving from Kinship Duna to the Fellowship of Play to Royals & Rogues changes context without replacing the mental model.

## Standard Studio frame

### Upper-left context and inspection

Always expose:

- Realm name and kind
- parent and hierarchy path
- current Scene or activity
- Source and active Avatar
- current roles
- current mode
- a single inspection entry for purpose, access, and relevant rules

This area orients; it does not become a creation toolbar.

### Center Field

The Field shows the current Realm, important relationships, active work, people and allies, child Realms, and a deliberately small number of next actions. Nested Realms use containment, breadcrumbs, labels, and ordered lists. They do not use a gravity or orbital layout.

### Right Ki panel

Ki persists across Realm and Scene transitions. The panel contains one conversation, speaker labels, the active gathering, optional voice, and context-appropriate suggested prompts. If focus moves into the Ki panel, the same input moves with it; a duplicate dock input must not remain active.

### Bottom action tray

Only context-valid actions appear. Creation begins conversationally with Ki. High-consequence actions use reviewable summaries and explicit confirmation.

## Realm 1: Kinship Duna

The Duna view presents purpose, participants, child Realms, activity, governance, resources, and three separate flows:

- Wisdom: knowledge with Source, context, visibility, contradiction, and provenance
- Presence: who or what is represented or active in the Realm
- Connections: relationships and lineage among Sources, Realms, invitations, Alliances, and participation

The Duna Mage is a Realm capability with a declared owner, purpose, scope, data access, actions, audit record, expiry or review, and revocation. It cannot enter private Realms or read private Wisdom.

## Realm 2: Fellowship of Play

The Fellowship retains the Studio frame and Duna administration patterns. Because it has few child Realms, it uses a stable, explicit list rather than a spatial constellation. A child card identifies Realm kind, name, state, Presence, activity, and the next action.

Creating a child Realm begins by asking Ki. Ki gathers purpose, participants, relationship to the parent, access, stewardship, visibility, and initial work; then presents a reviewable proposal.

## Realm 3: Royals & Rogues

The Studio frame remains Kiduna. Inside the Royals & Rogues Realm boundary, the Field adopts the game's quiet-enamel design system: deep navy, parchment, antique gold, thin cloisonné lines, and restrained light.

The Great Hall lists Cells and makes matching, invitations, voice, Allies, Actors, and game rules discoverable. The Cell builder asks for purpose, seats, access, matching intent, voice, duration, and any table variations.

## Gameplay sequence

The demonstration contains 15 states, each with a stable anchor:

1. Invitation received
2. Realm preview
3. Private Handshake
4. Great Hall arrival
5. Match preferences
6. Match proposal
7. Cell gathering
8. Table setup
9. Deal
10. Setup powers
11. Round
12. Counter
13. Showdown
14. After the hand
15. Realm return

Every state defines entry, primary decision, visible information, persisted context, exit, failure, and recovery. Progressive disclosure surfaces the rule needed now while the complete rules remain inspectable.

## Ki, Allies, and Actors

Ki may clarify, organize attention, draft, propose, and facilitate. Ki does not silently act as the Source, expand access, collapse contradictory Wisdom, reveal hidden game information, or acquire authority through proximity.

Bringing an Ally or Actor into a Realm requires owner, purpose, capability, information access, action access, visibility, duration, audit, and removal. Voice requires clear listening, speaking, mute, recording, transcript, and consent states.

## Success criteria

- Every major view has a stable URL.
- Every referenced story has a stable anchor.
- Realm nesting is obvious without reading documentation.
- Current Realm, Scene, Source/Avatar, role, and mode are inspectable.
- Ki remains available without becoming global navigation.
- One text input owns focus at a time.
- Permissions and consequences precede high-risk commitment.
- All interactive prototypes work with keyboard and visible focus.
- All source, docs, normalized data, and curated assets are downloadable.
